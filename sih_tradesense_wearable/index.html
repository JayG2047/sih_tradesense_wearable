    ⚠️

    The Problem

    Over 14,000 industrial injuries occur annually in India's skilled trades. Invisible steam jets, toxic sewer gases, and electrical arc flashes are the leading causes — and most are detectable seconds earlier with the right sensors.

    The Wearable Solution
